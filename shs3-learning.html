Why this design?

Practical learning
Activities are included because practical science was identified as a major performance gap.
Application
Questions move beyond recall toward application, analysis and scientific reasoning.
Feedback
Learners receive timely feedback and can be directed to remediation and retesting.
Connectivity
The production LMS can cache learning resources and queue offline activity for safe synchronization.
